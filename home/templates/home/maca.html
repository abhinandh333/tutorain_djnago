About MACA

MA's Charity Affiliate (MACA)

MACA (MA's Charity Affiliate) is a humanitarian initiative started with a simple but powerful mission — to ensure that no child is deprived of education due to life’s hardest circumstances.

Our first and most important phase focuses on providing completely free education to children who have lost both their father and mother. These children often face unimaginable challenges, and education becomes their only path toward a better future.

At MACA, we believe education is not a privilege — it is a basic right. Through this initiative, we aim to give these children not just schooling, but also hope, confidence, and the ability to rebuild their lives.

In collaboration with Tutorain, we provide access to:

  • ✔ Free online classes
  • ✔ Personal mentorship and guidance
  • ✔ Skill-based learning programs
  • ✔ Emotional and motivational support

This is just the beginning. MACA plans to expand its mission in future phases to support more underprivileged students and build a strong, educated community driven by compassion and opportunity.

Together, we are not just educating — we are transforming lives.

Our Vision

To create a world where every child, regardless of their background or loss, has access to quality education and the opportunity to succeed.

Our Mission

To provide free, accessible, and meaningful education to orphaned children and empower them with knowledge, skills, and confidence for a brighter future.

Apply for MACA Phase 1 Scholarship

Free Education Support for Students in Need

MACA Phase 1 Scholarship is dedicated to supporting students who have lost both parents. Through this program, we provide completely free education, mentorship, and guidance through Tutorain.

If you or someone you know is eligible, you can apply easily using the link below. Our team will carefully review each application.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✔ Student must have lost both father and mother
  • ✔ Must be willing to continue education
  • ✔ Basic access to mobile or internet (for online learning)
  • ✔ Genuine need for financial/educational support

Required Details for Application

  • ✔ Student Name
  • ✔ Age & Class
  • ✔ Contact Number
  • ✔ Guardian Details (if any)
  • ✔ Short background explanation
  • ✔ Upload or provide details of Death Certificates of both parents (for verification)

Terms & Conditions

  • ✔ All information provided must be true
  • ✔ MACA team may verify details before approval
  • ✔ Scholarship is subject to availability and review
  • ✔ Selected students must actively participate in learning
